About Hesam

Hesam is a skilled developer with over 10 years of professional experience across video games, digital media, HCI and immersive technologies (AR,VR,MR). He is also a lecturer on Immersive Technologies, Game Development and Programming. He believes in leveraging new technologies to transform dreams to reality! His passion is driven by his belief that XR, IoT & wearable tech alongside AI play a critical role in human evolution.

Over the past six years, I have immersed myself in projects at the intersection of technology, human perception, and interactive media, specializing in crafting compelling immersive experiences. My work bridges commercial, academic, and experimental domains, leveraging expertise in Unity3D, C#, and a diverse toolkit including Leap Motion, LIDAR, HTC Vive, Meta Quest, and Arduino. This technical foundation enables me to push boundaries and deliver innovative, user-driven interactions.

Selected Projects

Xeno Clinic

An immersive educational simulation for medical and laboratory science students offers a safe, controlled environment to practice procedures like ABO forward blood typing. It integrates optimized performance, fluid simulation, and precise tool logic with step-by-step guidance, real-time analysis, error detection, and adaptive audio–text feedback. Supported by a context-aware AI assistant and spatial UI, the experience enhances accuracy, reflective practice, and modern medical training.

Meetex

Meetex is an immersive VR platform that reimagines digital interaction through the lens of Digital Twin theory, fostering intuitive and human-centric communication in virtual spaces. Serving as a foundational step toward the Metaverse, it combines two core environments—an interactive multi-floor exhibition hall and professional smart meeting spaces—where users engage via customizable avatars, spatial audio, and real-time emotes. With robust support for multimedia content, seamless connectivity, and AI-driven assistants, Meetex delivers a scalable, metaverse-ready experience optimized for VR headsets and tailored for exhibitions, conferences, and enterprise collaboration.

Paykan

Paykan is a Serious Virtual Reality Game (SVRG) designed to help archers overcome Target Panic—a stress-induced performance block—by combining immersive gameplay with therapeutic techniques grounded in sports psychology. Developed with input from veteran coaches, the system features a realistic 3D archery simulation and uses a real recurve bow enhanced with a custom, lightweight, touch-sensitive shooting interface that wirelessly transmits data for real-time feedback. Through carefully designed levels and gameplay mechanics that balance engagement with performance training, Paykan offers a novel, science-backed approach to enhancing precision and mental resilience in archery.

Milk Master

A Gamified Virtual Reality Milking Simulator! Ever wondered what it’s like to milk a cow in Virtual Reality? In this fun VR game, you’ll pet, soothe, and milk a playful cow, trying to keep her happy while filling your bucket with as much milk as you can. Every pull counts, miss the bucket and the milk splashes straight onto the ground! With cheerful feedback, sound effects, and a barnyard atmosphere that’s both cozy and hilarious, this is farm life like you’ve never experienced before. Grab a stool, roll up your sleeves, and get ready to discover just how fun milking a cow can be!

Bimo

Bimo is an evocative 2D side-scrolling puzzle game set amidst the ruins of a once-vibrant city torn by war. Players guide a weary circus bear and a young boy through a hauntingly poetic journey, where every puzzle solved deepens the unspoken bond between the two. As they navigate decaying architecture and atmospheric landscapes, their contrasting strengths—raw instinct and intuitive grace—create a unique gameplay rhythm. The bear's stoic resilience and the boy’s quiet determination unfold without dialogue, relying on subtle environmental storytelling. With layered mechanics and visually driven narrative design, Bimo explores themes of loss, companionship, and quiet hope—transforming each interaction into an emotional footprint on a fractured world.

Tavandast

Tavandast is a set of four exergames developed specifically for elderly hand rehabilitation, combining therapeutic physical exercises with engaging, user-friendly gameplay. Built around clinically validated movement routines, the games leverage the Leap Motion Controller to enable accurate, natural hand tracking—completely free of gloves or handheld devices. Designed to promote both motor function and mental engagement, each game subtly challenges cognitive skills such as attention and focus, resulting in a motivating and accessible experience tailored to the unique needs of older adults.
